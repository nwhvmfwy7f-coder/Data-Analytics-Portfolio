# Retail Sales SQL Analysis

## Project Overview
This project uses PostgreSQL to analyze a simulated retail sales database containing 10,000 orders. The objective is to identify revenue trends, evaluate customer purchasing behavior, and measure product profitability.

## Tools Used
- PostgreSQL
- DBeaver
- SQL

## Dataset
- 1,500 customers
- 10,000 orders
- 17,440 order items
- 40 products

## SQL Skills Demonstrated
- SELECT and DISTINCT
- INNER JOIN
- GROUP BY and ORDER BY
- COUNT, SUM, and AVG
- DATE_TRUNC
- Revenue calculations
- Profit margin analysis
- Common Table Expressions (CTEs)

## Business Questions
The project investigates total orders, unique customers, revenue, average order value, product performance, regional sales, monthly trends, and profitability.

## Key Findings

### 1. Top-Performing Products
The retail sales analysis identified the following products as the highest revenue generators:

- **Bottle:** $142,464.06
- **Keyboard:** $134,937.72
- **Cable Organizer:** $132,467.49

These products were the company's strongest revenue performers in the simulated dataset.

### 2. Customer Purchasing Behavior
The analysis includes a ranking of the top 10 customers by total spending. This helps identify high-value customers and opportunities to improve customer retention.

### 3. Monthly Revenue Trends
Monthly revenue was analyzed across 2024 and 2025 to identify sales patterns and the highest-performing month. This analysis can help inform inventory and promotional planning.

## Business Recommendations

**Recommendation 1 — Prioritize High-Revenue Products**

Review inventory availability and customer demand for the highest-revenue products, particularly Bottle, Keyboard, and Cable Organizer, to reduce the risk of stockouts.

**Recommendation 2 — Evaluate Customer Retention Opportunities**

Use the top-customer spending analysis to identify potential loyalty and retention opportunities. Further analysis of purchase frequency and profitability would help determine which customers to prioritize.

**Recommendation 3 — Improve Sales Planning**

Use monthly revenue trends to guide inventory planning and marketing decisions. Investigate the reasons for monthly sales fluctuations before making changes to purchasing or promotional budgets.

### Highest-Revenue Month

December 2025 was the highest-performing sales month, generating **$225,606.72 in total revenue**.

This result identifies December 2025 as the strongest sales period in the simulated dataset. Further analysis could determine whether seasonal demand, promotional activity, or other factors contributed to the increase.

### Highest Profit Margin by Category

The **Accessories** category achieved the highest profit margin at **44.22%**, generating **$506,343.90 in revenue** and **$223,913.65 in profit**.

Although Home & Office generated more total revenue and profit, Accessories retained the highest percentage of revenue after product costs.

**Business Recommendation:** Investigate opportunities to expand sales of high-margin accessories while monitoring demand, inventory costs, and profitability. Compare product-level margins before making inventory investment decisions.

**Business Recommendation:** Review inventory planning and promotional strategies for high-demand months. Compare December performance with other months before making decisions about seasonal inventory investments.
